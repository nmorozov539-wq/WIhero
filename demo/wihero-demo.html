WI Hero demo

Both modes in one build, on the unified style. Example books only; nothing is saved.

Start here

  • ⌄ at the top left is the menu. Its first button switches between Normal and Hero mode (tap twice).
  • Normal opens on WIHero-Sample. Tap an entry; Mill, Storm, Tower and Gatehouse have a folded Story logic section with "Open in Hero mode".
  • Hero: the canvas. Tap a card for the inspector: every piece open and editable, plus Add piece. Drag a card by its six dots.
  • Preview (eye tab): a pretend exchange checked against the book. Tap a highlight for why.

Reading the canvas

  • Socket (notch) = in, knob (bump) = out. Links run knob to socket.
  • Teal opens: gates and gates-for. Red closes: close gates and closes-for, dashed with an arrow and bar. A red knob is a switch.
  • Yellow glue: an arrow is ride-along (Lantern rides with Tunnel); a yellow bar on the card's edge is hold (Storm).
  • AND / OR pills appear only where a gate has more than one item. Lock phrases hang off them.
  • Masks (Tower) are a stack of layers with ‹ main ›. Only the shown layer's links are drawn; a dashed hollow knob (Storm, Smithy) means its link goes to a hidden layer. Tap it to flip there.
  • Packs are dashed frames (#town) with their own ports. "all" or "any" sits on the link.
  • Links route around cards, never through them. Drag a port to another side of its card to move it.
