Hero mode

The second side of the editor: the same book, seen as a canvas of entries and story logic. One canvas per book.

Layout

  • Side panel (Obsidian style) lists the book's entries. Sort by folders, packs, A–Z or ID (sort button at the bottom). A dot means the entry is on the canvas.
  • Book picker at the bottom of the panel, and nothing else there. Switching books switches the canvas.
  • ⌄ menu (top left): Mode (Normal or Hero, plus "Open books in Hero mode"), Activation (Global and linked characters with Unlink), Settings (ST's World Info settings, this book's new entry defaults), then Rename, Export, Delete.
  • Canvas buttons by + and −: flow direction (⇄/⇅) and Fit.
  • Tabs work like the base editor. The first tab is always the Canvas; entries open in their own tabs with the full entry page.
  • Panel button on the canvas opens and closes the panel. The panel starts closed, with a hint the first time.
  • Preview tab (▷) is the third permanent tab.

Preview

  • A pretend exchange: one slot for your message, one for the character's reply. Tap a slot to edit it, ✕ to clear it. Nothing is saved.
  • It starts from the open chat's latest pair and how far its story has got. Tap the chip at the top and pick No chat open to start empty.
  • Highlights use the strategy colors: solid for entries with pieces, dotted for plain ones, 🔒 for lock phrases, struck through when blocked. Tap one to see what it did.
  • Also active lists permanent entries and vector matches, which have no phrase to highlight. Try "black bird" for Crows.
  • Report at the bottom: what fires and why, what's blocked and by what, and what would change.
  • Old hermit is set to triggered by you only, so the reply's "hermit" is blocked.

Try

  • Tap an entry in the panel: it opens in a new tab. ← or Back closes the tab.
  • The dot on the right is the canvas control. Filled: on the canvas, tap to jump there. Hollow: tap to place it in the middle of the view, or drag it where you want. While dragging, the canvas scrolls near its edges; drop on the ✕ circle to cancel.
  • Long press an entry to select. Then: On, Off, Move to a folder (or a new one), Edit… for parameters, Place, Pack, Add to a gate (one row of all selected), Delete with Undo. A folder's checkbox selects everything in it.
  • Left stripe = strategy (green keywords, blue permanent, purple vectorized). Off entries are dimmed and gray. Ferry is off.
  • Search and New entry sit in the panel's bottom bar.
  • ⠿ on a card: drag it to move the entry anywhere on the canvas. Changing the flow direction tidies everything back up.
  • Long press empty canvas: a picker with search adds an entry right there (or moves it there), or makes a new one.
  • Linked entries are always on the canvas. Add a link to an entry that isn't there and it appears. An entry with links can't be taken off; remove the links first.
  • Tap the canvas strip to close the panel. Switch to Ashfall Keep in the book picker to see a nearly empty canvas.

Ways in and out

  • In: book name ▾ → Hero in the base editor, or "Open books in Hero mode" (⌄ menu) to start here.
  • Back: closes a sheet, then the panel, then the focus, then an entry tab. On the canvas it closes the World Info drawer, like Back on Home. List and Hero are two sides of the same coin; Back never switches between them.

Pieces

  • In side: Gate (rows: each row = all of these; any finished row opens it). Out side: Gates for, Closes, Switch. Third side: Mask. Fourth side: Glue.
  • Packs are tags drawn as frames; readings (#town, #town:all, #town:2) live on the reader.
  • Locks: a gate row item that waits for a phrase in the newest user message or reply.
