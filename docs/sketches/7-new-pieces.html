5. Color vision modes (D22)

⌄ Display → Colors. Swatches in order: opens, closes, glue, mask, pack. Checked with a color-blindness simulation (Machado 2009): in Standard, opens and masks become nearly the same for red-green color blindness, which is why the modes exist. In each mode's own target, every pair stays clearly apart. Dashes and arrows still mark closes in every mode.
