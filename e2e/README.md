# End-to-end tests

`n1.mjs` plays a chat in a real SillyTavern 1.19.0 with a mock model and checks what reaches the prompt and the saved snapshots (BUILD_SPEC 13, N1): a chain advancing, a swipe and a delete rewinding it, a rider riding with its host, a lock in the user message opening in the same reply, a hold, a mask with a macro, the off switch, an old-build book, and a card round trip.

`n2.mjs` checks the editor at 390px wide (BUILD_SPEC 13, N2): the World Info button opening WI Hero, a book made, filled and organized without ST's editor (entries, condition, strategy, parameters, folders through selection, grouping, the on/off switch, Duplicate, Delete with Undo), search, a snapshot taken and restored with Undo and the "As imported" copy, problem badges, the color modes and content font, Global and Rename, Back and Escape, hiding and showing, deleting a book with Undo, four themes (screenshots in `e2e/log/`), "More settings" and a reload starting on Home. It makes and deletes the books "N2 Test" and "N2 Shelf".

`n3.mjs` checks Hero mode at 390px wide (BUILD_SPEC 13, N3): the sample book drawn once arranged (pack frame, OR junction, mask stack, links), a card and a port dragged, a mask layer flipped, the camera zoomed and panned, an entry placed from the panel, entry tabs with Show on canvas and Back, a pack sheet, Tidy with Undo, Show chat state against the open chat after two messages, a reload keeping positions, the moved port, the mask layer and the camera, and the mode switch both ways. It starts a new Seraphina chat and resets the sample book's canvas memory.

`n4.mjs` checks the inspector at 390px wide (BUILD_SPEC 13, N4): on a new book "N4 Test" every piece is added, edited and removed and checked in the saved book (packs, a gate with an entry, a pack reading, a lock and a wait, Story moves on, close gate, closes for, a mask whose text is written on its chip, gates for, who triggers, glue, switch, once); a port and the Problems sheet open the inspector; a greeting card takes Gates for and Closes for; and Reopen opens a closed entry and a swipe rewinds it. It deletes "N4 Test" at the end.

`n5.mjs` checks Preview at 390px wide (BUILD_SPEC 13, N5): seeded from the open chat after real turns, it fires exactly what the chat fired; and every block reason of CONCEPTS 7 shows up on the sample book (waiting on an entry, lock not armed, lock waiting for its phrase, wait not run out, closed earlier, Once, close gate met, closed this turn by another entry, the switch already picked, wrong side, turned off), with highlights that explain themselves, the report page, Also active, Fresh start and greeting starts, nothing saved, and Back. It starts a new Seraphina chat.

`n6.mjs` checks N6 at 390px wide (BUILD_SPEC 17): the chat bubble's count and names against the prompt; two replies with WI Hero off caught up oldest first from the popup; Not now leaving the choice on the bubble, and Keep as is from there; the bubble's switch; an untested version named, and a missing thread standing the engine down to plain World Info. It starts a new Seraphina chat and reloads at the end.

`n7.mjs` checks the piece bar at 390px wide (LAYOUT "Piece bar"): on a new book "N7 Test", the bar's chips, divider and fold; Opens by a tap and by a drag, its loose end dragged or picked, both or either; a line reopening its strip, ⇄ turning it around out of a shared row and Cancel putting the book back; "Road closes when Mill fires"; a loop and a self-close refused; a lock dropped on a line and a wait on its junction (ahead of the lock); a wait on a card and its pill's Remove; Glue Hold and Ride along; a switch's paths in order, waiting for the fork; a mask's name, on when and off when; a new nested pack and a link into it; and "+ Add logic". It deletes "N7 Test" at the end.

`n8.mjs` checks Moments at 390px wide (CONCEPTS 3.13): on a new global book "N8 Test", the Moment chip makes one on empty canvas (named, its phrases and who says it set in its inspector, stored off with no content) and one dropped on an entry (which it opens); Make it a Moment turns a lock into one, linked in the same row; the panel's Moments group lists them and the entry list, Normal mode and search leave them out; explain mode on a phone ("?", then a tap on a Moment card opens the Guide at Moment) and an inspector row's "?"; and in a real chat a Moment said in the user message opens its entry in the reply to it, while sending nothing itself. It deletes "N8 Test" at the end.

`desktop.mjs` checks the desktop layout (LAYOUT "Desktop: normal mode" and "Desktop: Hero mode") in the browser harness `e2e/desktop/harness.html`, a stand-in for SillyTavern's page with a made-up library: no SillyTavern needed, it serves the repo itself. At 1920 × 1080 (drawer 960px) and 1366 × 768 (683px): Normal mode in the drawer's box, Home's tiles, a book in two panes, a popover menu, Ctrl K; Hero opening widened with the entry list, canvas and inspector, the canvas left to right, hover handles and the delayed dimming, click to inspect and double-click to edit the text, the card menu, a link dragged from a handle into the strip and Escape putting the book back, Shift-drag selecting several, O arming Opens, explain mode (the "?" in the tab row, a lock pill opening the Guide at Lock, ? and Escape), Preview in two columns, an entry tab from the list, and Drawer size. Then a 600px drawer (the book opens on its list, an entry with ← back, Widen and an open side panel), a phone (nothing desktop shows) and the light theme. Screenshots go to `e2e/log/desktop-*.png`. Run `node e2e/desktop.mjs`; to look around by hand, serve the repo (`python3 -m http.server 8765`) and open `http://localhost:8765/e2e/desktop/harness.html` (`?reset` clears its saved settings, `?theme=light`, `?closed`).

`shape.mjs` checks shaping a link in the same harness, at 1366 × 768 and on a phone: a clicked line's handles, a ghost dot dragged into a dot, the line types by click and by keys 1–4, X to flip, a dot picked and removed with Delete and by a double-click, double-click on the line to add one, Cancel putting the shape back, a click on empty canvas as Done, the right-click menu's Reset shape, a card held at the view's edge scrolling it, and on a phone the 32px handles and the picked line brought into view above the strip. Run `node e2e/shape.mjs`; screenshots go to `e2e/log/shape-*.png`.

`cheap.mjs` checks the small additions in the same harness at 1366 × 768: Hero's + New book and Duplicate in the book picker, the keyword clash finder (⌄ and the entry page's marked key word), the dead branch report (⌄), lasso to make a pack (Shift-drag, then a name), sticky notes from the card menu, and the switch's Roll the dice. Run `node e2e/cheap.mjs`; screenshots go to `e2e/log/cheap-*.png`.

`perf.mjs` is a profile, not a check: at 6x CPU throttle and 390 × 844 it times Home settling, opening a book and an entry, scrolling, Hero mode, a card tap and a pan, and prints the long tasks. It makes 40 "Stress" books of 150 entries if they aren't there.

Run them against a throwaway SillyTavern, never your own: it changes the connection settings and the global lorebooks, and creates (then deletes) a character.

1. A SillyTavern 1.19.0 with fresh data (default Seraphina character), with this repo linked as `data/default-user/extensions/st-wihero`.
2. Copy `examples/WIHero-N1.json` and `examples/WIHero-Sample.json` to that SillyTavern's `data/default-user/worlds/`.
3. Start it (`node server.js --port 8000`, or set `ST_URL`).
4. `node e2e/mock.mjs &` (OpenAI-compatible mock on port 5001, logs prompts to `e2e/log/`).
5. `npm i --no-save playwright@1.55.0`, then `node e2e/n1.mjs`, `node e2e/n2.mjs`, `node e2e/n3.mjs`, `node e2e/n4.mjs`, `node e2e/n5.mjs`, `node e2e/n6.mjs`, `node e2e/n7.mjs` and `node e2e/n8.mjs`. Set `CHROMIUM=/path/to/chrome` if Playwright's own browser isn't installed.

The older scripts (gating, editor, labels, folders, visuals, workspace, phone, branches) test the old UI and are in the git history; their checks now live in n1–n8.
